# The Lord Considers the Lowly - சிறுமைப்பட்டவன்மேல் கர்த்தர் சிந்தையாய் இருப்பார்

This song of compassionate refuge and blessing echoes Psalm 41 — “Blessed is the one who considers the poor! The Lord delivers him in the day of trouble; the Lord sustains him on his sickbed; You have healed me.” It calls us to humility, considering the lowly, trusting God’s deliverance and healing. In the eternal day, this blessing is perfected — no more sickness, no more trouble, only eternal life with the Lamb (Revelation 21:4).
Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, who regards the lowly and heals the broken,
thank You for sustaining us in trouble and on sickbeds.
Let us be humble, consider others, trust Your deliverance.
In the eternal day, let us be blessed in Your presence forever.
Come quickly, our Sustainer and Healer.
